Privacy Policy for Re-Action

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nurlan Kuluev ("I", "me", or "the developer") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the mobile game Re-Action ("the game").

1. Contact

For any privacy question or request, contact me at nurlan.kuluev@gmail.com.

2. Information I collect

Hall of Fame (leaderboard) data. If you participate in the Hall of Fame, the game sends your chosen nickname, game progress, scores, and a unique player identifier to my server. This identifier links your leaderboard record and gameplay statistics to your player profile; it does not directly identify you by your real name.

This data is stored in a PostgreSQL database. The server and database are hosted using Railway infrastructure.

Server logs. The server and hosting provider may process technical information, such as your IP address and request time, for security and service operation.

I do not ask you to provide your real name, email address, phone number, precise location, or contacts for the leaderboard. Please do not use your real name or other personal information as your nickname, because your nickname and ranking data may be visible to other players.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game displays rewarded and interstitial advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Depending on your settings, region, and applicable consent choices, Google's advertising services may collect or process information such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data to deliver, measure, and personalize advertisements.

Learn more in Google's explanation of how it uses information from apps and sites that use its services.

On supported Android devices, you can manage advertising ID and advertising personalization settings through your device's privacy settings.

4. Advertising consent and privacy choices

Where required, the game uses Google's consent management tools to request consent or provide privacy choices concerning advertising. Depending on your region and the available options, you may be able to change or withdraw your choices through the "Privacy settings" option in the game's settings menu.

5. How I use information

  • To operate the Hall of Fame and display leaderboard rankings.
  • To associate player profiles with game progress and scores.
  • To maintain service security and help prevent abuse or cheating.
  • To support advertising that helps keep the game free.

6. Sharing

I do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate the game, including Railway for server and database hosting and Google for advertising services.

Your nickname and leaderboard data may be visible to other players when you participate in the Hall of Fame.

7. Data retention and account deletion

The game provides an option to delete the locally stored player profile and game progress from your device. This action does not delete your server-side leaderboard entry or associated gameplay statistics.

Request deletion of your player profile

To request deletion of your server-side player profile and associated leaderboard data, email nurlan.kuluev@gmail.com with the subject Re-Action Account Deletion.

Include your in-game nickname so I can identify the correct player record. I may request additional information reasonably necessary to verify the request.

When a valid request is processed, I will delete the associated player profile, leaderboard entry, scores, and stored gameplay statistics, except for information that must be retained for legitimate legal or security purposes.

Deletion requests will be processed within a reasonable period. Any information retained will be limited to what is necessary and kept only for the applicable retention period.

8. International transfers

Service providers may process or store information in countries other than your country of residence. Where applicable, appropriate safeguards will be used for international transfers of personal information.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live and the laws that apply, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information, restrict or object to certain processing, and withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To exercise your rights or ask a question about your information, contact me at nurlan.kuluev@gmail.com.

10. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact me so I can review the situation and take appropriate action.

11. Security

I use reasonable measures to protect information processed by the game. However, no method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Changes to this policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top indicates when it was last updated. The current version will be published on this page.
