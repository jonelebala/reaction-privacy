Privacy Policy for Reaction

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nurlan Kuluev ("I", "me", "the developer") handles information in the mobile game Reaction ("the game"). By playing the game you agree to this policy.

1. Contact

For any privacy question or request, contact me at nurlan.kuluev@gmail.com.

2. Information I collect

Hall of Fame (ranking) data. If you take part in the Hall of Fame, the game sends the following to my server: the nickname you choose, your game progress and scores, and an anonymous identifier used to link your progress to your nickname. This data is stored in a PostgreSQL database, accessed through an API hosted on Railway (railway.com).

Server logs. Like most online services, the server and hosting provider may process technical data such as your IP address and request time for security and operation of the service.

I do not ask for your real name, email address, phone number, precise location, or contacts. Please do not use your real name or personal information as your nickname, because nicknames and scores are shown publicly to other players in the Hall of Fame.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows rewarded and interstitial ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data to serve, measure, and personalize ads. Learn more in Google's policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

4. Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, or another region where consent is required, the game asks for your consent before ads are requested, using Google's consent management tools. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time through the "Privacy settings" option in the game's settings menu, where it is available. Residents of certain US states may also be offered choices about personalized advertising.

5. How I use information

  • To run the Hall of Fame and show rankings.
  • To save and restore your game progress.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse or cheating.
  • To show ads, which help keep the game free.

6. Sharing

I do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with service providers needed to operate the game: Railway (hosting of the server and database) and Google (advertising). Your nickname and ranking data are visible to other players in the Hall of Fame.

7. Data retention and deletion

7. Data Retention and Account Deletion Players can delete their locally stored profile and game progress through the game's settings. This action does not delete their server-side leaderboard data. To request deletion of their server-side player profile and associated data, players can email nurlan.kuluev@gmail.com with the subject "Re-Action Account Deletion" and provide their in-game nickname. Upon receiving a valid request, I will delete the associated player profile, leaderboard entry, scores, and stored gameplay statistics from the server, except for information that must be retained for legitimate legal or security purposes. Deletion requests will be processed within a reasonable period. Any retained information will be limited to what is necessary and kept only for the applicable retention period.

8. International transfers

Servers and service providers may be located in countries other than your own. By using the game you understand your data may be processed outside your country of residence.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to its processing, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, contact me using the email above.

10. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact me and I will delete it.

11. Security

I use reasonable measures to protect data, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

12. Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed. Continued use of the game after changes means you accept the updated policy.
